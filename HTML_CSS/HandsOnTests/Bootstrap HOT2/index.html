Popcorn Facts
Popcorn is a type of corn kernel that expands and puffs up when heated. It’s a whole grain and a healthy snack when prepared without too much butter or sugar!

Fun Fact: Popcorn was first discovered thousands of years ago and was even used in ancient celebrations.
